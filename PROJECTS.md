## Example of troubleshooting with proof

[The fix](https://github.com/damunozx123-debug/Patternx/commit/a7413db0b759650cdc8a3e605a6c6dac9b3b48c8) freed unused SDK components after compilation. [The next screenshot workflow passed](https://github.com/damunozx123-debug/Patternx/actions/runs/41729948322). This is **specific evidence of diagnosis, correction and verification**.
