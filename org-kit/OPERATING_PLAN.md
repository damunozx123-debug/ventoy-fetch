## Operating boundaries

1. Only diagnostic sessions on systems whose owners have clearly authorised the work.
2. Agree in advance on scope, fees, travel, customer data, deliverables and limitations.
3. Never present fictional demo measurements as client findings or unverified source code as a tested APK.
4. Keep customer evidence anonymised and publish testimonials only with explicit permission.
5. Seek specialist engineering review when skill limits, safety or reliability justify it.
6. Record actual acquisition, pilot revenue and time spent. Scale only on documented demand.
